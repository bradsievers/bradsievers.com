Therapy in the Des Moines Area

I’m Brad Sievers, a psychologist based in Johnston, Iowa, providing evidence-based therapy to adults in the greater Des Moines area and throughout the state. My office is located at Willow Grove Therapy Group on Northpark Drive in Johnston, just minutes from Des Moines, West Des Moines, Urbandale, and Ankeny. I also offer telehealth sessions to anyone located in Iowa.

About

Meet Dr. Sievers

I’m a licensed psychologist who provides individual therapy to adults across Iowa. I work with clients who are struggling with a range of mental health challenges, and I have advanced training and experience in treating post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD), obsessive-compulsive disorder (OCD), and anxiety disorders. I believe the foundation of effective therapy is a collaborative and trusting therapeutic relationship where clients’ goals are prioritized and their values and identities are respected and affirmed.

I practice at Willow Grove Therapy Group in Johnston, Iowa, and offer both in-person and telehealth sessions.

In-Person and Telehealth

I see clients in person at my office in Johnston, conveniently located for anyone in the Des Moines metro — including West Des Moines, Urbandale, Ankeny, Waukee, Clive, and surrounding communities. For those who prefer the flexibility of meeting from home, or who live elsewhere in Iowa, I offer telehealth sessions through a secure, HIPAA-compliant video platform. Research consistently shows that telehealth is as effective as in-person therapy for the conditions I treat.
